04 · ABOUT ME

Always learning.
Always building.

I’m Ravi Patel — an entrepreneur, business operator, and lifelong learner with a strong curiosity about business, technology, psychology, philosophy, politics, and the world around me.

I’ve always been interested in understanding how things work — whether it’s a business, a technology, a person, a government, or an idea. I enjoy asking questions, looking at problems from different perspectives, and trying to understand what could be done better.

My entrepreneurial journey has given me the opportunity to experience business from the ground level. I’m a co-founder of Dada Restaurants Ltd., where I’ve been involved in operating and growing a restaurant business.

At the same time, I’m looking toward the future. One of my long-term goals is to build a company in the technology and IT space.

Read more about me

I believe education should never stop. There is always something new to understand, question, explore, and improve.

Outside of business, I have a deep interest in Psychology and Philosophy. I enjoy learning about human behaviour, how people think, why we make the decisions we make, and the ideas that shape the way we see the world.

I’m also interested in politics and public policy. I’m fascinated by how governments work, how different ministries function, how policies affect people's everyday lives, and how public systems can be improved.

Through my personal YouTube channel, I plan to explore these subjects in depth. One goal is to create series where I take a particular ministry, department, policy, or public system, learn how it works, examine its challenges, and think critically about what could potentially be improved.

I consider myself open-minded and willing to try new things. I welcome suggestions, different opinions, and ideas that challenge the way I think.

I don’t have everything figured out. My goal is to keep learning, keep building, stay curious, and hopefully create something meaningful along the way.

Learn. Explore. Question. Improve.
